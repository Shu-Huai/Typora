# 1109-PR-D-25-02913_R1_reviewer

This paper proposes a multi-task privileged learning framework integrating knowledge distillation, conducts experiments on several tabular and one image dataset. The results demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms existing LUPI and distillation baselines. It also analyzes the causes of misclassification errors in detail. The authors have made detailed revisions in response to the previous reviewers’ comments. The following are some additional suggestions.

1. The authors did not included the reason for the statement "In some cases, the privileged model may achieve better generalization, resulting in superior performance compared to the teacher model (A < C)." in  Section 4.
2. The authors used 5-fold cross-validation on the tabular datasets but an 80:20 split on the image dataset, without explaining the reason for this difference, which may cause misunderstanding.
3. Essentially, MTP is a type of setup characterized by "multimodality during training and unimodality during testing". It is highly related to "modality hallucination / missing-modality learning / privileged labels as hints", but the associated discussions are relatively brief.
4. The authors select a single feature as the privileged information based on its Spearman correlation with the label. Readers might be concerned that this approach could lead to overfitting to specific statistical patterns.
