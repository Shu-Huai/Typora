# 0609-SPL-42893-2025_Proof_hi.pdf

This paper proposes a Sign-Aware Graph Learning (SAGL) framework for stance detection in social networks, addressing the common challenge of missing link sign information. The framework comprises two key components: SIGAE for infering latent signs (positive/negative) of user interactions by leveraging stance labels and graph structure and BSGAN for refining node representations by capturing and dynamically adjusting the influence of inferred signed links. Experiments on Twitter and MGTAB datasets demonstrate the superiority of this method. Overall, it is sufficiently innovative and experimental, but there are the following areas for improvement.

1. There is an error in the abbreviation in the abstract: "SAGAE infers and generates signed links from unsigned networks...."
2. The construction of the semantic features matrix is not mentioned in the article. It is recommended to supplement the details of feature extraction.
3. The symbol style of "loss" in Equation 18 is incorrect. Please check if there are any other spelling mistakes and formatting issues in the thesis.
4. Some formulas in Chapter III are somewhat confusing. It is advisable to transform them into a clearer form or enhance the explanations of these formulas.
5. The decision to threshold the sign prediction at a fixed value θ is heuristic. Please explain how and why θ is chosen and whether alternative strategies were considered.
6. The integration of balance theory into the model somewhat appears abrupt and under-motivated.
