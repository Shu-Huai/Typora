This paper proposes a Sign-Aware Graph Learning (SAGL) framework for stance detection in social networks, addressing the common challenge of missing link sign information. The framework comprises two key components: SIGAE for infering latent signs (positive/negative) of user interactions by leveraging stance labels and graph structure and BSGAN for refining node representations by capturing and dynamically adjusting the influence of inferred signed links. Experiments on Twitter and MGTAB datasets demonstrate the superiority of this method. Overall, it is sufficiently innovative and experimental, but there are the following areas for improvement.
