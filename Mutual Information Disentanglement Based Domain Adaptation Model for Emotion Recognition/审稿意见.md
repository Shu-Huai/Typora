Reviewer: 2

Recommendation: R - Reject (Paper Is Not Of Sufficient Quality Or Novelty To Be Published In This Transactions)

Comments: This work addressed the domain generalization problem in EEG-based emotion recognition task. In order to tackle this issue authors adopted a framework which employs a source domain classifier and mutual information (MI) estimator module to disentangle domain unrelated feature (e.g., nuisance factors) from domain related features by minimizing the mutual information. Experiments on the SEED and SEED-IV datasets demonstrate significant improvements in categorical emotion recognition performance.

1.  Improving generalization capability by employing MI minimization criterion has already been adopted in various speech processing (e.g., speaker recognition) and other applications. In this regard, the technical novelty of this work is somewhat limited. However, the authors apply this approach specifically to EEG-based emotion recognition, making the overall framework, as presented in Fig. 1, a novel contribution to this domain.
2.  The proposed framework, as shown in Fig. 1, was not adequately described. For instance, the feature extraction module was not discussed, and the specific architectures used for the domain-related and domain-unrelated encoders were not specified.
3.  The authors should provide sufficient information in the caption of Fig. 1 to make it self-explanatory. This includes defining the variables and acronyms used in the figure to ensure clarity and ease of understanding.
4.  The ablation study appears incomplete, as the authors did not examine the impact of the domain classifier (L\_{dom}). Including this analysis would provide a clearer understanding of its contribution to the overall framework.
5.  The following statement is unclear and confusing “For the cross-subject classification, the data of 15 subjects in the same session are taken as the source domains and target domain”

Please re-phrase this statement.

6.  Similarly, rephrase the following statement “... for the cross-session classification, the data of three sessions of the same subject are taken as the source domains and target domain.”
7.  The experiments were conducted on the same dataset (i.e., SEED or SEED-IV), with results reported for cross-subject and cross-session scenarios. This experimental design is somewhat limited. To better demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach, the authors should consider reporting cross-dataset results.

**审稿人2**

**建议：R - 拒绝（论文质量或创新性不足，无法在本期刊上发表）**

评论： 该工作解决了基于EEG的情感识别任务中的领域泛化问题。为了解决这个问题，作者采用了一个框架，使用源领域分类器和互信息（MI）估计模块，通过最小化互信息来分离领域无关特征（例如干扰因素）和领域相关特征。SEED和SEED-IV数据集上的实验显示了情感识别性能的显著提升。

1.  通过采用MI最小化准则来改善泛化能力已经在各种语音处理（如说话人识别）和其他应用中得到了采用。在这方面，本文的技术创新性是有限的。然而，作者将这种方法专门应用于基于EEG的情感识别，使得图1中展示的整体框架在该领域成为一种新贡献。
2.  图1中展示的框架没有充分描述。例如，特征提取模块没有讨论，领域相关和领域无关编码器所使用的具体架构没有说明。
3.  作者应在图1的说明中提供足够的信息，以使其自解释。这包括定义图中使用的变量和缩写，以确保清晰易懂。
4.  消融研究似乎不完整，因为作者没有检查领域分类器（L\_{dom}）的影响。加入这一分析将有助于更清楚地理解其对整体框架的贡献。
5.  以下声明不清晰且令人困惑：“对于跨受试者分类，同一会话中15名受试者的数据作为源领域和目标领域。”

请重新表述此声明。

1.  同样，请重新表述以下声明：“...对于跨会话分类，同一受试者的三次会话数据作为源领域和目标领域。”
2.  实验仅在同一数据集（即SEED或SEED-IV）上进行，结果仅报告了跨受试者和跨会话场景。此实验设计有些局限。为了更好地展示所提出方法的有效性，作者应考虑报告跨数据集的结果。
