Reviewer: 2

Recommendation: AQ - Publish In Minor, Required Changes

Comments:
This paper proposed a multi-source domain adaptation model (MSDA-MID) based on mutual information disentanglement for the electroencephalogram emotion recognition Task. It also introduced multiple the losses to constrain the model training. The methods were evaluated on the SEED and SEED-IV datasets with the cross-subject and cross-session scenarios. The results show the effectiveness of the proposed methods to a limited degree.

There are some comments or concerns that need to be explained or addressed.

1.The overall novelty of the paper is limited. Lots of domain adaptation methods including the mutual information based ones have been attempted in many areas. Mutual information is also used for emotion recognition [1][2], even with disentangle motivation [3].

2.The domain adaptation scenario is limited, as Reviewer#2 pointed in Comment7, “The experiments were conducted on the same dataset (i.e., SEED or SEED-IV), with results reported for cross-subject and cross-session scenarios. This experimental design is somewhat limited. To better demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach, the authors should consider reporting cross-dataset results.” The revised paper supplemented the cross-dataset evaluations. Actually, such work is really more meaningful. What the readers may be more interested in a scenario that the proposed method improved the cross-dataset performance where baseline methods perform poorly in cross-dataset setting while the proposed method tackles this problem.

3.The baselines are relatively old and non-directly related. Latest baseline methods are very few, although the original one of the revised paper may be submitted in 2024. This paper used 6 comparison methods. The methods DDC [19] (published in 2014), DAN[21] (published in 2015), and CCORAL[22] (published in 2015), MS-MDA[23](published in 2021), MISDA [24] (published in 2022), and MS-ADA[25](published in 2023).
First of all, the first three used the dataset Office-31, a collection of images from three distinct domains: Amazon, DSLR, and Webcam. They are not new and not-directly related to this EEG emotion recognition. The last three are EEG emotion recognition directly related, but they are not very new.

4.The title of the paper “Mutual Information Disentanglement Based Domain Adaptation Model for Emotion Recognition” is not very proper. This is not a general method about domain adaptation model for emotion recognition. The domain shift is a more generic challenge for emotion recognition, such as different datasets and different recording environment. However, this paper only focused on EEG-based emotion recognition with cross-subject and cross-session scenarios. I would suggest adding some qualifiers to the title, such as EEG, or other information that specifies the specific task or testing scenarios. (Please compare with the titles of [23, 24, 25].)

## 创新问题

跨被试识别和跨时段识别是经典的问题。

由于不同被试或者同一被试在不同时段的脑电信号存在显著差异，在跨被试或跨时段任务中需要引入域适应技术。

在我们的任务中，源域一般有多个，且之间也存在着分布不同的问题。多源域适应方法通过同时考虑多个源域与目标域之间的分布差异，充分学习多个源域的可迁移知识，因此应该使用多源域适应方法。

MS-MDA 利用最大均值差异（MMD）对多个源域与目标域的边缘分布进行一对一对齐，能够在一定程度上缓解源域之间以及源域与目标域之间的分布差异。

MS-ADA 在 MS-MDA 的基础上增强源域与目标域之间的相关性，并对多个分类器分配权重以获得更可靠的结果。

MISDA 在此基础上引入了共享提取器和分类器，不仅一对一地实现域对齐，也在所有源域的公共特征上与目标域对齐。

但这些方法都要求在 N 个源域上设计 N 个分类器。

我们设计了一个只使用两个分支的模型。我们使用互信息最小化机制将域无关特征与域相关特征解耦，并使用原型分类

互信息衡量的是两个变量之间的共享信息。在多源域适应任务中，域无关特征与域相关特征如果存在较高的互信息，说明两者在表达空间中存在显著的信息耦合，导致域无关特征无法完全剥离域特定信息，从而影响模型的跨域泛化能力。
